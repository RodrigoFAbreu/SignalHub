4A friend's first run, and the empty and error states

Someone you invite starts with nothing: no events, no producers, no idea what SignalHub is. These screens take them from the invitation to their first subscription, and say plainly what went wrong when a code expires or a device is removed. Setup, welcome and the expired-code screens come before the app is connected, so they have no bottom bar; every main screen after that shows it. The empty Producers tab is built on the search-and-chips layout: 4d no producers at all, 4g nothing under Yours, 4h nothing under Can subscribe. The server's operator is called "the host" everywhere; no screen names a person as the server's admin.
