3Names: taken, not allowed, and renaming

A producer name is shared by everyone on the server, even for a private producer, and it must follow the server's rule (letters, digits and . _ : / -, starting with a letter or number, up to 100 characters). Both errors show under the field as the person types, on Create and in the rename dialog alike, and Create or Rename stays off until the name is valid. Renaming changes the name everyone sees; the events stay with the producer.

5No working key, and a producer that is disabled

Two things can stop a producer: its keys, and being disabled. 2n shows every key revoked. 2o is disabled by you (you can enable it). 2p is disabled by the host (the person running the server): you can't enable it, only ask. Each says what stopped and what to do, in one banner, and the same words reach the owner's list rows (the Producers page, 1g). Other people's disabled producers read only “Disabled”.

6A producer that isn't available, and the subscription row

When you lose sight of a producer (it was made private) it leaves your list without a notice. If you had its screen open, or tap an old push or link, the server answers 404 and the app says so plainly, with one way back (2q). An event that is no longer in your inbox, most often because you unsubscribed from its producer, has one screen only: 4o on the First run and empty states page (the former 2r was folded into it). 2g1 to 2g3 are the subscription row of 2g in its other states.
