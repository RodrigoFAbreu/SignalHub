7The web page: the same app, laid out for a big screen

The web page is the app in a browser: the same tabs, rows, wording and colors, so nothing has to be learnt twice. On a wide screen the bottom bar becomes a navigation rail and lists open their item beside them (list and detail); at phone width it is the app's layout. People is for admins only: the rail and the bottom bar do not show it to anyone else (7g). The Producers view follows the app's Producers tab (search plus filter chips All, Yours, Subscribed, Can subscribe; "Yours" is both the chip and the tag on a row), and a signed-in account menu with Sign out sits at the bottom of the rail on a desktop and at the top right on a phone. A producer's address uses its id (#producers/01997d60-2f4b-7c3a…), because a name can change when it is renamed. Settings, Devices, People and the key shown once are in Web - Settings, people and states. It only uses its own scripts and fonts it serves itself, so Roboto and the icons are bundled, not loaded from Google. Touch targets are 48 px or more (a 32 px chip has a 48 px hit area). Frames 7a to 7f sign in as Rodrigo (Admin); 7g signs in as Rui (Basic). Icon-only controls have accessible names: the account button is "Account menu, Rodrigo", the list's icons "Filter events" and "Mark all as read", the pencil "Rename NAME"; the account menu closes on Escape and returns focus to its button.
