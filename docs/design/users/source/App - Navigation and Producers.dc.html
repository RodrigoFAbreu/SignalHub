1Bottom navigation and the Producers tab

A bottom navigation bar. Everyone gets Inbox · Producers · Settings; admins also get People (4 destinations, within Material's 3 to 5). The unread count is a badge on the Inbox tab, and the inbox app bar has no gear icon since Settings is a tab. Producers is search plus filter chips (1d): one list, narrowed by All, Yours, Subscribed and Can subscribe, with a New producer button. 1f is the no-results state. Every icon button, the bell included, has a 48dp target. The bar is 80dp high plus the system navigation inset; the New producer button sits 16dp above it.

3Producer rows in every state, and subscribing in place

1g is the Producers list with every kind of row. Losing access is silent: when an owner removes you from a private producer, or makes it private, the row simply disappears from the list (no notice, no empty slot) and your subscription is gone; if the owner adds you back, you subscribe again yourself. 1h to 1j are the bell: in flight, failed, and undone. Subscribing and unsubscribing are undone with a snackbar, never a dialog (they are idempotent and nothing is lost). 1n is the list at 360dp wide and 200 % text.

4The Inbox tab's unread badge, from 1 to over 99

The badge counts every unread event of the producers you are subscribed to, whether or not a push was muted or paused on this device. Up to 99 it shows the number; above that it shows 99+ and stops growing, so it never leaves the tab. The screen-reader label gives the real count: “Inbox, 124 unread”. With nothing unread the badge is absent.

5“See its events”: the inbox for one producer

“See its events” (on a producer's screen) opens the inbox narrowed to that producer. It is the same list, with the producer as a chip you can remove and the producer's name as the title. Only producers you are subscribed to can be filtered; the inbox's producer filter lists those and no others.
